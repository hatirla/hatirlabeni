Hatırla Beni
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Türkçe · Terms of use · Support

In short: Hatırla Beni (“Remember Me”) is an app that helps people of all ages, especially older adults and their families, keep their memory and mind active. The app runs entirely on your phone. Family photos, names, voice recordings, health notes, reminders and game results are stored only on this phone; they are not sent to the developer or to any other server. You do not need an account. There are no ads, no ad tracking and no usage analytics.

2. Information stored on your phone

You or your family enter the following information. It is kept only in the app’s own storage on your phone. The developer cannot access it.

  • Names, relationships, short notes and photos of family members and acquaintances
  • Voice recordings made by your family
  • Song titles, song files and optional photos
  • Reminders (medicine, water, etc.), their times and optional photos
  • “To the person who finds me” details: phone numbers of relatives and an optional health information note
  • Game results and activity history (for the doctor’s report)
  • Settings and the settings passcode (stored only as an irreversible hash)

The health information field is optional. If you fill it in, it stays on your phone and is shown only on the Help screen.

3. Information that leaves your phone

The app sends information off the phone only in the following cases.

a) Help button

If the person using the app chooses to message a relative from the Help screen, the app gets the current location and opens the phone’s own WhatsApp or SMS app with a ready-made message. The message is sent only when “Send” is pressed, to the person you chose. It does not pass through the developer’s servers. Location is requested only on this screen; there is no background location tracking.

b) Subscriptions (Plus and the Doctor’s report add-on)

If you buy a Plus subscription or the Doctor’s report add-on (available to Plus members), payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the app never sees your card details. The RevenueCat service is used to verify the subscription status. Only the following is shared with RevenueCat:

  • A random user ID created by the app, not linked to your name
  • Purchase information (plan, dates, subscription status) and the store receipt
  • Technical information (device model, operating system version, country/currency)

Family information, photos, voice recordings and health notes are never sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

c) Backup file

When you make a backup, all information is collected into a single file that you save where you choose (e.g. Google Drive, iCloud Drive, email). The app never sends this file anywhere by itself. The backup file is not encrypted and contains photos and health notes; please keep it only somewhere you trust and do not share it.

d) Doctor’s report

If you choose to share the doctor’s report, it is sent through the phone’s share sheet to the app or person you choose.

5. Ads, tracking and analytics

There are no ads in the app. No tools are used to track you across other apps or websites. No usage statistics or crash reports are collected.

6. Retention and deletion

You can change or delete the information on your phone at any time inside the app (Settings → Delete data). When you uninstall the app, all information stored in it is deleted as well (except backup files you made, which you delete yourself). Subscription records are kept by Apple / Google and RevenueCat according to their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults and their families. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

The only personal data processed by the developer is the subscription information described in section 3(b), processed to provide and verify your subscription (legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations). It is shared with Apple Inc., Google LLC and RevenueCat, Inc., whose servers are mainly in the United States. You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact: hirudolab@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days.

9. Health notice

Hatırla Beni is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis or treatment. The doctor’s report is only supporting information for a conversation with your doctor. Always consult your doctor about health decisions.
